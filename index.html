PDEs · Computational Mathematics · Machine Learning

Pitambar Acharya

I study mathematical and computational problems arising in nonlinear partial differential equations, with broader interests in data science and scientific machine learning.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied and Computational Mathematics at the University of Alabama at Birmingham, advised by Prof. Yanni Zeng (expected graduation Spring 2027). My research focuses on global existence and stability for chemotaxis models with logistic growth and logarithmic sensitivity. I am also an experienced instructor of Intermediate Algebra, Precalculus, Calculus and Probability/Statistics.

Research

Partial Differential Equations · Mathematical Biology & Chemotaxis · Data Science · Machine Learning · Scientific Computing

Partial Differential Equations

Global existence and asymptotic stability for chemotaxis models and hyperbolic–parabolic systems, with emphasis on initial–boundary value problems on the half-line and quarter plane.

Computational Mathematics

Modeling and analysis of transport phenomena: advection–diffusion with variable diffusivity and contaminant dispersion in turbulent atmospheric flows.

Data Science & Machine Learning

Machine learning for turbulence prediction, spectral embedding on data graphs, adaptive filtering, and operator learning for PDEs.

Dissertation

Global Existence of Solutions on the Quarter Plane for a Chemotaxis Model with Logistic Growth and Logarithmic Sensitivity

The work combines pointwise Green's function estimates in the Liu–Zeng / Liu–Yu framework with a Nakamura–Nishibata boundary-cancellation energy method extended to the fully nonlinear setting.

Advisor: Prof. Yanni Zeng · University of Alabama at Birmingham
